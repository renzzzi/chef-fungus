	Station Class Inheritance Tree:
		Station -> HoldableStation -> Blender
								   -> Chopping Board
								   -> Oven
								   -> Stove
								
	Station Scripts Explanation:
		station.gd:
			Only stores class_name, station_ui_interact_component variable and the
			getter for the component.
		
		station_slot:
			Attached to a UI slot that can store food and sends a signal if its
			area was pressed with mouse left-click. Also handles the food image
			within itself to match whatever it's storing.
			
		station_ui:
			Holds the logic of swapping food between what the player is holding or
			lack thereof and the slot interacted with by the mouse.
			
		holdable_station.gd:
			A child class of the Station class that is responsible for turning
			station_ui_interact_component on or off accordingly for the holdable
			station this script is attached to.
	
	Components:
		interact_component.gd:
			Register and unregisters itself within the Player's nearby_interact_components 
			array and sends a signal when interact() has been called by player.
			This is attached to all objects that can be interacted by the player.
	
		holdable_component.gd:
			Attached to objects that can be held by the player. It handles following
			the player, knowing if it is picked up, being placed inside or taken 
			out from stations and on counter-tops.
	
		station_ui_interact_component.gd:
			Attached to stations with UIs and is responsible for detecting if they
			are interacted with and opens/closes the UI accordingly. For Holdable
			Stations, this component is disabled until they are placed on a
			counter-top.
	
	Player:
		The Player is responsible for interacting with everything, picking up objects,
		and initiating processes in stations. It is the most referenced in scripts.

ALL ABOUT FOOD:
	Food Tiers:
		This is based on how they were prepared and what they were combined with.
	
		Raw Ingredient -> Basic Dish/Ingredient -> Intermediate Dish -> Final Dish
	
		They do not necessarily have to go through each tier.
		(e.g. Two Raw Ingredients can become a Final Dish immediately)
	
	Food States:
		Food change states based on which station it was processed through. Below
		are suffixes for these food states.
	
		- Chopped (Chopping Board)
		- Blended (Blender)
		- Baked (Oven)
		- Boiled (Stove)
	
		Note: For Raw Ingredients and Final Dishes, they do not have these 
		suffixes.
	
	Food Freshness:
		Food has a timer where if a certain threshold has passed, food will degrade
		its freshness worse and worse as time goes by. Below are the three states of
		freshness a food can be in.
	
		Fresh -> Stale -> Spoiled
	
		Once food transitions to the next state of freshness, it can never go back.
		Placing food in/on any station will slow down spoiling with varying 
		effectiveness. Food's freshness affects overall quality if served/prepared.
	
	Food Categories:
		Food are categorized by a general classification like Meat, Dairy, Fish,
		Spice, Grain, Vegetable, Fruit, etc. Food can have multiple classifications.
	
	Sludge:
		This is the output when you combine food that does not exist in the game's
		recipe logs. They will produce repulsive sludge that is not fit for serving.
		Be careful when preparing Final Dishes on a station because they have a 99%
		chance of turning into sludge.


ALL ABOUT STATIONS:
	Fridge:
		It has 15 slots where you can store food to slow down spoilage
		by 90%.
	
	Counter-top:
		This is an essential station to be able to place holdable stations such as
		the Blender, Chopping Board, Mortar, Oven, and the Stove to be able to prepare 
		food on them. You may also place a singular piece of food on the counter 
		to slow down spoilage by 25%.
	
	Blender:
		You can blend a singular piece of food to turn it into its Blended state.
		Needs a tamper to operate on.
	
	Chopping Board:
		Chop a singular piece of food to turn it into its Chopped state. Needs a 
		knife to operate on.
	
	Oven:
		Bake a singular piece of food to turn it into its Baked state. You may also
		combine and bake 2-3 foods to create a new dish. Needs a sheet pan to operate
		on.
	
	Stove:
		Boil a singular piece of food to tunr it into its Boiled state. You may also
		combine and boil 2-3 foods to create a new dish. Needs a cooking pot to
		operate on.


ALL ABOUT TOOLS:
	Tools are required to operate on certain stations, they wear down every use and
	you might have to get a new one if its worn down. Below are the existing tools
	and the station they are used for.
	
	- Tamper (Blender)
	- Knife (Chopping Board)
	- Sheet Pan (Oven)
	- Cooking Pot (Stove)
